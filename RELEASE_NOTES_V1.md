# AuthorityLens v1 — Pilot-Ready Research Prototype

## Scope

AuthorityLens v1 packages a synthetic authority-state engine, deterministic consequence and control views, an interactive four-scenario browser demo, a structured representation benchmark, and a pilot-ready exploratory human-study design.

The research identity is usable security / HCI built on agent-authorization concepts. This is not a new authorization protocol or new revocation semantics.

## Evidence available

- Tests for the frozen engine, views, demo, benchmark, and local pilot-session operations.
- Forty deterministic synthetic benchmark scenarios, with 32 confirmed-revoke post-action scenarios eligible for structured view-model comparison.
- A reproducible representation report and machine-readable results.
- A 16-item pilot task bank and one-condition, nine-trial session schedules.

The benchmark reports exact structured fact matching under the prototype taxonomy. It does not establish what is visibly rendered in every view, what people understand, or whether anyone makes better decisions.

## Evidence not available

No human-subject study has been conducted. There are no human-comprehension, usability, preference, cognitive-load, decision-quality, safety, or real-world deployment findings. No real Gmail service, external agent, asynchronous operation, provider acknowledgement, or remote revocation is connected.

## Known limitations

- The state model and scenario set are synthetic, small, and structurally simplified.
- Independent survival of an issued delegation is an explicit, non-universal prototype assumption.
- Queued effects and unknown external states are represented as static inputs, not executed processes.
- Benchmark extractors share engine state and vocabulary; this is specification-coupled validation, not an independent semantic oracle.
- The structured comparison includes view-model fields that are not all printed in default cards.
- The pilot materials remain preparation only. The participant information/consent page is a placeholder; researcher authentication is a local prototype boundary, not production security.

## Future empirical step

After replacing the placeholder with approved participant materials and determining applicable institutional ethics / IRB requirements, an exploratory study could compare target-local and consequence-oriented views on controlled scenarios. It should measure factual identification, task-defined false-safe and false-alarm responses, response time, and optional confidence. The study should report null or adverse outcomes as directly as positive ones.
