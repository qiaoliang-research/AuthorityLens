# AuthorityLens related-work matrix

**Review date:** 2026-09-30. This is an evidence-anchored comparison of selected sources located in a purposive review, not a systematic review. “NO EVIDENCE FOUND” means that the inspected source did not describe that dimension; it is not a claim that the dimension is absent from all related work. “NOT A FOCUS” means the source’s stated question is elsewhere. Source type and status are part of the comparison: recent arXiv submissions and Internet-Drafts are not peer-reviewed conference findings or adopted standards.

## Reading the matrix

1. The model-level vocabulary—delegation graphs, revocation propagation, residual rights, pending work, and unknown/indeterminate closure—is established or directly covered by current technical work. AuthorityLens must present these as modeled inputs and an integration, not as new authorization semantics.
2. Permission interfaces, access reviews, contextual delegation, and user comprehension have substantial prior work. “Target-local versus system-level context” is not a new general interface principle.
3. The reviewed primary human studies do not directly compare the frozen AuthorityLens pair on the combined post-revoke AI-agent case containing residual descendants, queued effects, and unresolved remote status. This is a bounded search observation, not a novelty finding. The project’s proposed human question remains open and may overlap work not found here.
4. The closest overall technical overlap is Santos-Grueiro’s EffectBound preprint for truthful effect-closure claims at an interface boundary. Watts’s draft has the broadest direct state-model overlap, while Abak’s draft is a close evidence model for control delivery and uncertain outcomes. These are not user-comprehension studies, and the Internet-Drafts are work in progress rather than adopted standards.
